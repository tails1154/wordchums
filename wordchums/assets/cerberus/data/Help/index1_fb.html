Multiply your points!

Long words are great, but multiplier squares deserve your real attention. Find a way to make a word across more than one and you're likely to see some really big points. Playing across two W2 squares multiplies your points times 4! Playing across a W3 and a W2 multiplies your points times 6!

Resist playing a word that sets up your opponent to use the multipliers against you!

Unless the word you're playing is giving you some REALLY big points. If there's another word that gives you pretty good points and does not set your opponent up to play on a W3 square next turn, go with that instead.

Use hint after you find a good word

How do you know you've found a good word? A green points badge shows that you're at least half way to the best word on the board. The Hint feature points to the location of a word that's at least half way between your highest word and that best word on the board.

Bomb low points!

Use a bomb when the points are low and the badge is already green. The bomb gets you a new set of letter tiles and lets you keep your turn.

Premium Stars and Best Word

The 4th star shows that your word is at least 75% of the best score and helps you zero in on that max score. It will also signal Best Word when you find the best points. Premium stars may be purchased in the Shop under the Boosts tab.

2-letter power words

Combining high-point letters (J,Q,X,Z) with just one vowel can score big points. Placing a big letter on a multiplier square that’s part of two words scores mega points.

Hooking!

You can often place letters such as S (or a blank, or E) at the end of an existing word..then make a whole new perpendicular word. Veteran players call this “hooking”. Many words can also be “hooked” with a new starting letter. Add an E in front of SPECIAL and you have ESPECIAL.

Play adjacent words!

Play adjacent to another word to earn points for every word that’s made. Letters that form a new word vertically and horizontally get counted twice! Imagine a Q (10 points) on a triple letter that’s part of words up and down…that’s 60 points for just the Q!

Don’t get stuck with big points!

If your opponent makes the last play of the game, all of your remaining letter points are SUBTRACTED from your score AND ADDED to theirs!

Level Faster

What’s XP?

You start out earning 1 XP (experience point) for every point in your word. The level bar at bottom of screen shows you how many points you need to get to the next level.

Gear gets to the next level faster

Hats and Hats and holds increase XP by the bonus listed on that item. For example…if you are wearing a hat with XP +15% and a hold with XP + 9% then you add those together and get 25% more experience for every word. Note: You get the XP bonus only for the gear you are wearing.

Energy Boost

Energy doubles your experience rate. It also doubles the gear. Combining gear and boost will soon have you more than tripling the XP you earn each turn!
