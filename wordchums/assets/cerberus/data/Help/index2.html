Tap screen elements for help

Back

Press back to leave this game.
  • TIP: When there are multiple turns to play you can swipe to the next game

Pass

Pass is used to skip your turn. It’s typically used at the end of a game when you can’t find anywhere to play a letter.

Bomb

Tap Bomb to blow back all 7 of your letter tiles and receive 7 new ones.
  • TIP: Great to use when you have all consonants or vowels or when the point badge turns green on a low number

Hint

Tap Hint to highlight a square that can be used to make an excellent word. The word will cross the hint square in the direction of the arrows. The hint word will always have more points than any word you’ve already found.

Game Menu

Game Menu has the following options:
  • Help
  • Swap & Pass – Pick letter tiles to throw back and get new ones. Then it’s the other player’s turn.
  • Resign – End the game with a loss
  • Help – More detailed help
  • Feedback and Support

Center Square

The first word of the game must cross this sun square.

Multiplier Squares

Multiply the value of a letter or of an entire word. For example: L3 multiplies letter points x 3. W2 multiplies the word points x 2.
  • TIP: Crossing multiple multipliers scores big points

Shuffle/Recall

Recall button pulls all letters off the board. It will Shuffle the letters when they’re all in the rack.

Page Dots

A green dot is shown for every game in which it’s your turn. They turn red after a play in that game.

Opponent’s Play

Shows the results of your friend’s last turn. Most of the time it shows the word and score but it can also say SWAPPED or PASSED.

Your Score

Your current score in this game.

Your Chum

Tapping your chum shows your inventory, stats and achievements.

Chat

Tap on chat to send a message to your friend. The chat button highlights when you receive a chat.

Letter Pile

Number of letter tiles remaining to be drawn. The game ends when a player uses all their letters and there are none left to draw.

Friend’s Chum

Tapping a friend’s chum shows their gear, stats and achievements. Eyeglasses below a chum show they are online and looking at game.

Friend’s Score

Shows your opponent’s score and the number on the left side of sign is their level.

Level and XP bar

Your level, current XP (eXperience Points) and the XP to reach the next level. You earn one XP for every point in your word. Gear and Energy Boost give you big XP bonuses. The bar is blue when an Energy drink is active.

Coins

Your current coin balance. Earn 1 coin for every tile played.

Point Badge

The point badge calculates the points for the word. It turns GREEN when the points are over halfway to the best possible score.

Dictionary

Tap a word on the board to see its definition.

Word Power Stars

Two stars when the current word's points are 25% or more of the best word. Three stars show you have more than 50% of the best possible score.
